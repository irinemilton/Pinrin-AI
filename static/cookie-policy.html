Cookie Policy

Pinrin AI uses only the storage and browser technologies required to operate the experience and remember local session state.

How We Use Storage

Pinrin AI may use browser storage to remember interface state, campaign progress, or preferences. The current frontend may use local storage for non-sensitive session information.

Essential Technologies

Essential storage helps the site load correctly, maintain workflow state, and connect the frontend to the configured backend. These technologies are not used to sell personal information.

Third-Party Services

When enabled and authorized, Pinrin AI can connect to services such as Pinterest, Gumroad, Hugging Face, and hosting providers. Those services may have their own cookies, logs, and privacy policies.

Analytics

Platform analytics are only displayed when returned by authorized integrations. Pinrin AI does not fabricate analytics or infer activity that the connected services do not provide.

Your Choices

You can clear browser storage through your browser settings. Clearing storage may reset local workflow state. For questions about data or privacy, contact irinemilton009@gmail.com.
