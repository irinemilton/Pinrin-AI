Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Pinrin AI is a personal AI-powered application developed and operated for the developer's own use. It supports workflows related to idea research, digital product development, creative asset generation, and automation through connected services.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when Pinrin AI operates, including when it connects to third-party services or uses their APIs.

Although the application is intended primarily for personal use and is not currently offered as a public service to multiple users, it may process information through external providers to perform its intended functions.

2. Information We May Process

Campaign and Product Information

Campaign instructions, product ideas, product metadata, descriptions, pricing details, and other information used to research, develop, or manage digital products.

Uploaded Files and Creative Assets

Product documents, images, design assets, generated content, and other files supplied to or created through application workflows.

Workflow and Publication Records

Agent execution events, workflow status, audit records, publication history, generated artifact metadata, and technical logs needed to operate or troubleshoot workflows.

Pinterest Information

When Pinterest integration is enabled and authorized, the application may access information permitted by the granted API permissions. Depending on the enabled features, this may include authorized account information, boards, Pins, or publication-related information.

The application does not claim access to information that Pinterest has not made available through its authorized APIs.

Technical Information

Hosting providers and connected services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, request logs, and error reports according to their configurations and policies.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

  • Operate the application's personal workflows.
  • Research and organize product ideas.
  • Generate requested text, descriptions, and creative assets.
  • Validate files and manage digital product information.
  • Execute authorized operations through connected services.
  • Publish or manage content when supported and authorized.
  • Maintain workflow records and diagnose technical problems.
  • Protect the application and its credentials against unauthorized access.

Information is processed for the application's intended purposes and in accordance with applicable service requirements.

4. Artificial Intelligence Processing

Pinrin AI may use third-party AI services to interpret instructions, generate content, assist with research, and support automated workflows.

When these services are used, relevant prompts, files, metadata, or other information may be transmitted to the selected provider to perform the requested operation.

Each AI provider handles information under its own applicable terms, privacy policy, and data-handling practices.

The information transmitted depends on the particular workflow and the provider's integration.

Pinrin AI does not use Pinterest API data to train or improve AI or machine-learning models unless Pinterest expressly permits that use.

5. Pinterest API Integration

Pinrin AI may connect to Pinterest through its official developer platform, subject to the permissions and API access granted by Pinterest.

When the integration is enabled and authorized, Pinterest information may be processed to support permitted features, such as working with authorized boards, Pins, or publication workflows.

The application will use Pinterest API data only for permitted purposes and in accordance with applicable Pinterest developer requirements.

Pinterest authorization tokens and other credentials should be protected and must not be exposed in publicly accessible source code or browser-delivered application code.

Access to Pinterest functionality depends on the permissions granted and the availability of the relevant API features.

For more information, review the Pinterest Privacy Policy and Pinterest Developer Guidelines.

6. Third-Party Services

Pinrin AI may use third-party services to provide its functionality, including:

  • Pinterest: For authorized Pinterest API functionality.
  • Gumroad: For supported digital product workflows, if enabled.
  • Hugging Face: For model access or related AI functionality, if enabled.
  • AI providers: For processing prompts and generating requested content.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: For deploying and operating the application.

Information may be transmitted to a provider when necessary to perform an operation requested through the application.

The specific providers and information involved depend on the deployed configuration. References to a service do not mean that every integration is necessarily active.

Third-party providers independently manage their services and process information under their own applicable terms and policies.

7. Credentials and Security

Pinrin AI may use API keys, access tokens, and other credentials to communicate with connected services.

These credentials should be stored securely, preferably in server-side environment variables or an appropriate secrets-management system.

The application is intended to avoid exposing provider secrets in browser-delivered code. Actual security depends on the implementation and deployment configuration.

Reasonable precautions should be taken to protect credentials, restrict permissions, prevent unauthorized access, and revoke credentials that are no longer required.

No online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Information Sharing

Pinrin AI is a personal-use application and is not intended to sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services when necessary to execute an enabled workflow, such as generating AI content, accessing Pinterest functionality, managing digital product information, or hosting the application.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where disclosure is necessary to address security issues or protect legitimate rights.

The information shared depends on the requested operation, the integration involved, and the permissions granted.

9. Storage and Retention

Depending on the application's configuration, information may be stored locally or through the configured backend, database, hosting platform, or connected service.

Stored information may include campaign state, workflow events, publication records, generated artifact metadata, and technical logs.

Retention periods depend on the storage configuration and the policies of the providers involved.

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended purpose, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate operational needs.

Information may remain temporarily in backups, logs, or third-party systems under the applicable retention policies of those services.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Depending on the implementation, Pinrin AI or its hosting and authentication providers may use cookies or similar technologies for session management, authentication, security, preferences, or other necessary functionality.

The actual technologies used depend on the deployed application.

Where legally required, appropriate disclosures and choices will be provided for non-essential cookies.

Browser settings can be used to manage cookies, although disabling certain cookies may affect functionality.
