Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains the information Pinrin AI may process when you use the platform.

Information We May Process

Depending on the enabled workflow, this may include campaign instructions, product metadata, uploaded product files, creative assets, agent run events, publication records, and support messages.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the workflow, generate requested drafts, validate files, publish approved content through authorized integrations, provide support, and improve reliability.

Third-Party Integrations

When you authorize a provider, relevant information may be sent to that provider to perform the requested operation. Pinterest, Gumroad, Hugging Face, and hosting providers process information under their own policies.

Credentials

API keys and access tokens should remain server-side. Pinrin AI does not intentionally expose provider secrets in the browser.

Storage and Retention

Campaign state, audit events, publication records, and generated artifact metadata may be stored by the configured backend. Retention depends on the deployment storage configuration.

Security

No online system can guarantee absolute security. Use strong credentials, restrict provider permissions, and do not upload information you are not authorized to process.
